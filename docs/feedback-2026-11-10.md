# Hub rules from the client (10 Nov 2026)

Source: Loom "CTA Challenge Hub, Feedback and Testing Plan"
(https://www.loom.com/share/4483ca281a2d484eb0d8b4782f54bbf2) and the client's Notion page
"Feedback: CTA Challenge Hub"
(https://app.notion.com/p/travelamore/Feedback-CTA-Challenge-Hub-40df2e2d77aa802dbf9ce009a93d20a0).
The client redesigned the Hub in their prototype and asked for it to be copied exactly. Where this
file and docs/brief-2026-10.md disagree, this file wins.

## Leaderboards
- [ ] Follower Growth (month, top 3): followers since the 1st; ▲/▼ rank change since yesterday across all students, "–" if unchanged.
- [ ] Most Viral Posts (last 7 days, top 5): ranked by views; the number/arrow opens the post.
- [ ] NEW tag: post was not in yesterday's top 5; the tag stays for **48 hours** after it first appears.
- [ ] Total Posts (month, top 5): ties share a rank, first to reach the number listed first; always 5 rows, "👥 +N" on the 5th opens the others.
- [ ] Countdown "X days left", switching to hours on the last day.
- [ ] A click on an IG handle **always opens their Instagram page**.

## Student page (one page, toggle)
- [ ] Title follows the toggle: "100 Post Challenge" / "CTA Passport". Challenge is the default.
- [ ] Bottom note, one line on desktop: "Changed your Instagram handle? Tell us here, and your posts and passport stamps stay with you."

## Passport
- [ ] Stamp progress bar: "Stamps", gold bar, "11/22"; animates from 0 each time the passport opens.
- [ ] Booklet: dark navy leather cover, page edges on the outer sides, fold shadow and dashed stitching on the spine; cream paper with wavy security lines; visa pages have a large faint plane watermark.
- [ ] "Creator Takeoff Academy" centred on every page; page numbers from page 2.
- [ ] Desktop: two-page spreads. Mobile: one page at a time.
- [ ] Page 1: "Creator Passport" + small gold plane crest; silhouette photo; Handle, Type "Creator", Member since (= Takeoff stamp date), Stamps "11 / 22", Issued by "Creator Takeoff Academy".
- [ ] Signature of holder: capitalised name as a signature, on a straight line, left-aligned under the label.
- [ ] MRZ, two lines, 44 chars, monospace: `P<CRT<<JULIAN<BOES<<<…` and `11<22<<FEB2026<<CREATOR<TAKEOFF<ACADEMY<<<…`.
- [ ] Stamps oldest first from page 2; **max 5 per page**, left page fills first; 6 positions in a loose 2×3 grid, off-grid, tilted up to ±17°, varying ink strength, may overlap; **stable per member**.
- [ ] Always an even number of pages (blank page at the end if needed).
- [ ] Takeoff is given on the join day: always the first stamp, top position of page 2.
- [ ] Follower stamps the member already qualifies for at joining are given immediately, dated with the join month.
- [ ] Stamps are permanent.
- [ ] Page turning like a book: drag/swipe the right page left (forward) or the left page right (back); 3D rotation around the spine following the pointer; completes past ~1/3, else falls back; page curves and casts a shadow.
- [ ] Arrows ‹ › with "Pages 1–2 of 4" (desktop) / "Page 1 of 4" (mobile), same animation; left/right arrow keys on desktop.
- [ ] Vertical scrolling on mobile keeps working with a finger on the passport.
- [ ] Stamps "thud" on one after another each time the passport opens (large and transparent → small bounce), ~140 ms apart. Stamps tilt on hover.

## Stamps you can collect
- [ ] A card headed "Stamps you can collect": every stamp not collected yet, smaller and slightly faded, in its category colour.
- [ ] Lock in the centre: dark circle, gold border, gently pulsing.
- [ ] Name in bold, description below starting with a capital letter.
- [ ] Hover: lifts and tilts.
- [ ] Rows, always this order (desktop up to 6 per row, mobile 3; partial rows centred; empty rows disappear):
  1. Takeoff, Silver Wings, Golden Wings, Co-Pilot, Captain, First Captain
  2. Wanderer, Explorer, Nomad, Globetrotter, **Pioneer**, Legend
  3. **Economy Plus**, Business Class, First Class, Hidden Gem, Landmark, Wonder of the World
  4. Boarding Pass, Jet Setter, Frequent Flyer, Around the World

## 100 Post Challenge
- [ ] Counts every feed post on/after the join date (Reels, carousels, images); not Stories or Trial Reels; collabs count for both; no daily cap; **deleted or archived posts stay counted**; capped at 100 for the challenge only.
- [ ] Rewards at 10, 15, 30, 35, 50, 55, 70, 75, 90, 95, 100; Heartbeat message sent exactly once; reward log (milestone, reached, sent). 100 also gives "Around the World".
- [ ] Levels: "Level = 1 + milestones reached". (The Notion examples "Level 2 at 20 posts … Level 10 at 100" don't fit that formula; the client's prototype shows Level 2 at 11 posts, so the formula is what's built. Level 12 at 100.)
- [ ] Stats row: Level, Total posts, Posts this month (IST).
- [ ] "X / 100 posts" + progress bar: counts up from 0 and the bar fills along, slowing at the end like a slot machine.
- [ ] Next reward line: "You have just N posts to go until your next reward! 🤩" / "You have just 1 post to go until your next reward! 🤩" / "You did it! All 100 posts done 🏆".
- [ ] Grid 10×10: orange = not yet, green ✓ = posted; milestones show a gift, styled differently; 100 = trophy, larger, glowing, "Post 100" caption below.
- [ ] Gift-opening animation the first time a milestone circle is reached.
- [ ] "🎁 Rewards along the way", short text about Heartbeat, chips "🎁 Post 10" …; reached ones highlighted.
- [ ] Note under the grid: "Every feed post since you joined counts (reels, carousels, static posts). Stories aren’t included."

## Testing plan
- Part A: 25 travel creators (list below), one week of normal daily updates. Check post counts (Reels, carousels, images; no Stories/Trial Reels), only posts from the join date, follower stamps given at joining, growth/views/posts stamps, leaderboards incl. ties.
- Part A also checks: only posts on/after the join date count; follower stamps given at joining (dated with the join month); growth, views and posts stamps as creators reach them; all three leaderboards incl. ties.
- Part B: two fresh Instagram test accounts (Test Student A and B), each linked to its own Heartbeat account (Julian's and Apeksha's), join date = first day of the test. Checks: day one Takeoff + right "Member since"; first post → Boarding Pass; reaching 10 → gift circle, level up, Heartbeat message with the right text; multiple posts per day all count; two milestones in one update → two separate messages; running the update twice sends nothing twice; a collab between A and B counts for both and both get Co-Pilot; a deleted post doesn't lower the count; a Story and a Trial Reel don't count; a handle change keeps posts, stamps and level and the new handle is found by search; the "Changed your Instagram handle?" link reaches the team; phone and computer, incl. swiping passport pages.
- Done when: every Part A and B item passes, spot checks match Instagram, every expected Heartbeat message arrived exactly once.
- The current accounts are deleted before the 25 are added (client's request, 10 Nov).
