## Screens shown in the Loom

### Student Hub (public, no login): warm beige/cream, orange, rounded hand-lettered headings, travel theme
- **Home** ("CTA Challenge Hub"): orange card "Search your name to open your 100 Post Challenge &
  Passport" with an Instagram-handle field and a Search button. Below: "Leaderboards".
  - Follower Growth: podium, top 3 (gold/silver/bronze circles), handle, +gain, "followers",
    a ▲/▼ rank-change pill. Subtitle "October · 18 days left".
  - Most Viral Posts ("Last 7 days"): top 5, handle, NEW tag, views + ↗ link to the post.
  - Total Posts ("October · 18 days left"): top 5, handle, "N posts".
  - Footer: "Updated … ago".
- **Search is exact-handle only**: no autocomplete, so students can't browse each other
  (data isn't confidential, but it shouldn't be easy to look others up).
- **100 Post Challenge page**: "N / 100 posts", tabs "100 Post Challenge | Passport",
  "N posts in October". A 10×10 grid of orange numbered circles: green ✓ for done, navy circles
  with a gift icon at reward steps, a trophy at 100. Section "🎁 Rewards along the way".
- **Passport page**: "← Back to leaderboards", "Passport", "IG Handle: @…", the same tabs.
  An open passport book. Left page: "CREATOR PASSPORT" header, photo, Name, Type "Creator",
  Member since "Feb 2026", Stamps "11/22", Issued by "Creator Takeoff Academy", signature, and a
  machine-readable line ("P<CRT<NAME…"). Right page: inked stamps (e.g. "+100 followers",
  "10K", "1K"). Below the book: "Stamps you can collect".
- The Hub is shared as **one link on Heartbeat**. Open question from the client: a way for students
  to land on their own page directly instead of searching every time (bookmarking works).

### Student Stats (team, one shared login): same beige/orange look, left sidebar
- Sidebar: Overview · Students · Needs attention (red count badge) · Leaderboard · Viral Reels ·
  Troubleshooting. Top: "Search students", "Updated 2d ago". Footer "Updated Oct 12, 2026 at 6:00 AM IST".
- **Overview** ("Community performance by month", month picker "October 2026 (to Oct 12)"):
  - KPI cards: Avg. followers gained per student (+660, ▼ vs Sep 12), Avg. posts per student,
    Avg. Heartbeat logins per student, Students in the community (100, +joined/−left).
  - Needs attention summary: "Not seen on Heartbeat for 14+ days", "No post for 14+ days",
    "Contract up for renewal within 30 days" (each with a count, "View all").
  - Follower Growth top 5 and Top Posts top 5 (views, ↗).
  - Charts: Avg. follower growth per month and Avg. posts per month (Nov 2025 – Oct 2026,
    current month in a stronger colour); Follower growth range and Posting range (bucket histograms).
- **Students**: "100 active · 206 former", Active / Former / All, filter by name or handle.
  Columns: Student (handle, name, Instagram ↗), Followers today, Growth this month, Posts this month,
  No post for, Last seen on Heartbeat, Last contact, Contract ends in (or "Left Jul 2025").
- **Needs attention** (count badge): filter chips Last seen / No post / Contract, thresholds
  7+ d / 14+ d / 30+ d / 60+ d / All. Columns: Last seen (Heartbeat), No post for, Posts, Contract
  ends, Followers, Last contact (hover shows the last note).
- **Student detail**: breadcrumb "Needs attention › Name", prev/next "1 of 23", links to Instagram
  and the Heartbeat member page, "Member since Jan 2026 · 9 mo · 6-month contract · 95 days left".
  Month picker listing every month. KPI cards with the student's rank ("#3 of 100") and change:
  Followers, Follower growth, Last seen on Heartbeat, Posts, Avg. posts per month, Days since
  last post. "Follower history" chart (All time / This year / …). Contact log with "Add note";
  the latest note shows when hovering "Last contact" elsewhere.
- **Leaderboard** (long version of the Hub boards): Rank by Followers / Follower growth / Posts;
  Period 1 day / 7 days / 30 days / This month. Columns Followers, Growth, Posts, Move (▲/▼).
- **Viral Reels**: top 10 with "Show all" (top 100): rank, handle, date, views, likes, "View ↗".
- **Troubleshooting**: left open ("depending on how you want to build it"), with a re-run button
  and clear per-student "workflow failing and how to fix it".

## Other points from the Loom
- Rewards: when a scrape shows a student reached a reward step, they get an **automatic message on
  Heartbeat** (via Zapier / Make / whatever works) with the prize (a Notion page, an artifact link,
  a voucher…).
- "The level of detail in the prototypes can be copied; the design can be done better."
- Tool stack matters: the agency will add many more projects, so they want one long-term stack,
  not lots of tools.
- If this is a lot more work than originally agreed, they're open to discussing compensation.
- Meetings: Fridays 8:00–12:00 IST.

## Written briefing (as pasted)

See the conversation of 28 Oct 2026; key rules:
- Daily update at 06:00 IST; show "Updated X hours ago".
- Per student: name, IG handle + link, Heartbeat member page link, join/leave date, contract end and
  length (6/12 months), fixed internal ID.
- Instagram daily: followers per day; every new feed post (date, format, link); reel views daily for
  7 days, day 7 = final, only finals for viral lists; no Trial Reels; collabs count for both; no stories.
- Heartbeat: logins per student per day (or total per day), last seen.
- Contact log (date, text, author).
- Community size: current count, +joined / −left per month; left students hidden from live lists,
  history kept; stop scraping on leave.
- Past months frozen. Current month partial; compare with the same days of the previous month and
  mark it incomplete in charts. Scrape failures visible ("no data since X days").
- Averages per student who was in the community that month (open: how to count end/start-of-month
  kickoffs).
- Hub: active students only; IST month boundaries; Follower Growth top 3 with daily rank change
  across all students; Most Viral (7 days, top 5, Reels/videos unless other views are obtainable,
  link to post, NEW tag); Total Posts (month, top 5, ties share rank, earlier reacher first, "👥 +N");
  countdown (days, hours on the last day); exact-handle search incl. old handles; username-change
  form that updates the database; 100 Posts from join date with rewards at
  10/15/30/35/50/55/70/75/90/95/100 and a send log; posts this month; Passport with 22 permanent
  stamps stored with month/year, in earned order, 5–6 per page, exact names/texts, signature from the
  real name.
- Stamps: Takeoff, Silver Wings (6 mo), Golden Wings (1 yr); Boarding Pass (first post after
  joining), Jet Setter (15 posts in a month), Frequent Flyer (10+ posts 3 months in a row);
  Wanderer 1K, Explorer 5K, Nomad 10K, Globetrotter 25K, Trailblazer 50K, Legend 100K followers;
  Economy Premium +100, Business Class +1K, First Class +10K followers in a month; Hidden Gem 10K,
  Landmark 100K, Wonder of the World 1M views on one post (day-7 count); Around the World (100 Post
  Challenge done); Co-Pilot (first collab); Captain (top 3 on any leaderboard), First Captain (#1)
  — at month end or any daily update.
